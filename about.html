About Me

Fashion Designer & Computer Science Student blending technical precision with creative vision to craft unique, innovative designs.

Abba Isa Mohammed

I am a passionate fashion designer and computer science student based in Jalingo, Taraba State. My unique journey combines the analytical thinking of computer science with the creative expression of fashion design, allowing me to approach design problems with innovative solutions.

My work is characterized by a fusion of traditional Nigerian aesthetics with contemporary design principles, creating pieces that are both culturally resonant and modern. Operating from Nassarawo Main Market in Jalingo, I draw inspiration from the rich cultural heritage of Taraba State and its diverse communities.

As a student at Taraba State University, I'm constantly exploring how technology can enhance the fashion industry - from digital pattern making to e-commerce solutions for local designers. This dual expertise allows me to bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern technology.

Blending traditional Nigerian textile artistry with contemporary fashion trends to create timeless pieces that honor heritage while embracing modernity.
