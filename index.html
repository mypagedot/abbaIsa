Creative Innovation

Welcome to my world of design, where traditional craftsmanship meets cutting-edge technology. As both a fashion designer and computer science student, I bring a unique perspective to every project.

Based in Jalingo, Taraba State, I specialize in creating custom fashion pieces that honor Nigerian heritage while incorporating modern design principles and technical precision.

My workshop at Nassarawo Main Market serves as both a creative studio and a hub for exploring how technology can transform the fashion industry in our local community.
